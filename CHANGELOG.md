# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. Format loosely follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## 3.0.0 — 2026-10-21

The playbook narrows to what it does well: proposing, designing, auditing and visualizing A/B tests. It no longer analyzes results or computes sample size, duration, significance or a winner; a request for any of these gets one out-of-scope line, and the user's testing tool does that work. Every proposal also becomes a compact scenario record instead of a regenerated three-box write-up, so a turn reads, repeats and writes less. A card is delivered only through quality gates: code checks what code can check, two reviewers check what code can't, and a failed check is an error with its reason, never a warning that lets delivery continue. And the card shows only the comparison: the test's question, the two mockups large and side by side, the change ringed and named.

### Added — quality gates
- **Review stamps and fingerprints.** `validate_scenario_json.py --fingerprint` prints the record's SHA-256 fingerprint (without `review` and `card`); scenario-critic's PASS writes it into `review.scenario_critic`. `build_card.py --draft` needs that stamp, writes a draft marked "TASLAK — incelenmedi" / "DRAFT — not reviewed" (with `abtest-status` and `abtest-final-fingerprint` meta tags), a manifest of every change inside the rings, and prints the final card's fingerprint; mockup-reviewer's PASS writes it into `review.mockup_reviewer`. The final build writes the card only when both stamps match; a refusal writes nothing, names the failed gate and says an existing output file wasn't updated. Only PASS is ever stamped.
- **Card checks that refuse:** any A/B difference outside the rings; a numeric difference (price, amount, percentage, count, rating) anywhere without a `numeric_change` reason; legal or mandatory text, or verification and security text, that A shows and B drops; a consent box unticked in A and ticked in B; an interactive component B adds without an accessibility warning; values shaped like personal data (national ID, IBAN, card number, e-mail, mobile number; masks and all-zero placeholders pass).
- **Record checks that refuse:** an interactive change without an accessibility warning carrying its criteria (touch target, contrast, keyboard, screen reader; focus and close for a modal; pause for an auto-advancing carousel); a functional change without `invariants`; a revenue primary on a change that touches a user benefit (coupon, discount, campaign, free shipping, refund, cancellation) without a guardrail on that benefit; an `adapted_from` that isn't an archive title, or an archive or adapted record whose variable or difference doesn't match its archive block; user-data or sector evidence without a note; intuition at high priority; "tested", "proven", "won" or an invented chance or uplift percentage without user data, and "guaranteed" ever; urgency or scarcity wording in B without a `regulation` warning naming the real data source. The keyword lists are named constants, matched on word boundaries and documented as heuristics.
- **Schema:** `invariants` (1-5 one-sentence functions that must work identically in both arms), `numeric_change`, `review` (PASS stamps with 64-hex fingerprints).
- **Methodology:** a Quality gates section with the responsibility table, the delivery pipeline, fingerprints and what the gates can't guarantee; functional equivalence under Variable isolation; "a revenue win must not hide user harm" under KPI rules; accessibility criteria per element. `mockup-style.md`: draft and final card, the manifest, numeric differences, personal-data patterns, accessibility for added components.
- **Tests:** a negative and a positive case for every gate, reproducing the earlier escapes (a price differing outside the ring, a second change inside the ring, a card built on warnings, the coupon revenue case, unreviewed and stale final builds).
- **Evals:** the coupon harm case (02), archive evidence never presented as tested and delivery only after both reviews (05), a second change inside the ring caught by the mockup reviewer and an interactive addition with its accessibility criteria (06).

### Removed
- The statistics engine (`scripts/analyze_results.py`) and its tests: significance, sample size, power, duration, guardrail margins, segment interaction, revenue metrics, SRM, CUPED, bootstrap and the Bayesian view.
- The results skill (`ab-test-results`) and the results examples and evals (`examples/results-walkthrough.md`, `evals/04-results.md`, `evals/09-results-prereg.md`).
- The statistical pre-registration block (`preregistration`, `sample` in the scenario JSON, `--prereg-only` in the validator), decision rules, guardrail tolerance margins and reading windows, and the A/A test branch.
- Numeric ICE scoring (`ice` in the scenario JSON and the "ICE: Orta (6×5×8)" card tag).
- Top-level `rationale`, `hypothesis` (`change`, `mechanism`, `expected_direction`), `test_items`, `kpis` and `dont_items` in produced scenarios. The mechanism is a top-level field, B's description is the change, the direction sits in `primary_kpi`, and the three boxes moved to the optional `detail`, written only on request.
- **Everything on the card except the comparison**, from the template and the builder (not hidden with CSS): the text column, the description, the summary, KPI and warnings boxes and the classic three boxes, the footer with its source, evidence, objection and priority tags, KPI pills, the brand note, the mockup-basis disclaimer, footer notes, shift notes and the "Stronger alternative:" footer line. No record field other than the title is printed.
- `card.brand_note`, `card.footer_notes`, `card.shift_note_a` and `card.shift_note_b`; a removal no longer needs a shift note (A's ring label names the removal, B is genuinely empty).
- The record field `history`: nothing produced or read it; the history lives in the user's `.abtest-history.md` and a tested variable is stated in chat.
- The legacy flat card input (`examples/scenario-card-input.json`, top-level `desc`, `kpi_items`, …) and its builder path; only scenario records build.
- The orange, teal, amber and navy box palette.

### Changed
- **Priority replaces ICE:** a tier (high / medium / low) with a one-sentence rationale that weighs impact, evidence and ease in words. The chat tag line prints "Öncelik: Orta" / "Priority: Medium". A strong candidate passes the mechanism gate and has priority medium or high.
- **Setup notes replace the setup spec and pre-registration:** target audience, where the variant appears, exclusions and concurrent tests, pre-start gates; the primary KPI and the guardrail live in the record and aren't repeated. No split, sample, duration or decision rule.
- **Guardrails** stay mandatory, measure an independent harm and state their denominator; they carry no numeric margin. A lagging outcome names its follow-up window inside the KPI name. Accessibility stays a pass/fail check closed before launch.
- **Regulated flows:** compliance sign-off is a pre-start gate in the setup notes and a `regulation` warning in the record (one Never-do item in the detailed view); the validator refuses a regulated-looking record without that warning.
- **Test memory:** the user keeps `.abtest-history.md` from their own tool's results; the playbook reads it to avoid re-proposing a tested variable and never writes or re-interprets it. A tested variable is stated in chat with its recorded outcome; the record carries no `history` field.
- **Audit** reviews UX, CRO, methodology, ethics, privacy, regulation, accessibility and feasibility; statistical claims in a plan get one "outside scope, not checked" line.
- Rule 5 is now "No statistics"; rule 19 has nine question kinds (the traffic question is gone).
- **The scenario record:** every produced scenario is one compact JSON record with ten pieces of information: `title`, `problem`, a one-sentence `mechanism`, `variable`, `variants` with `difference`, `primary_kpi` (name, denominator, direction), `guardrail` (a metric with denominator and direction, or a check with a criterion), `source` / `adapted_from` / `source_note` / `evidence`, `priority`, and `warnings` only when something critical applies. The validator refuses a generated record without `source_note` and a guardrail named like the primary, and warns on a multi-sentence problem or mechanism, a missing "?" and over-confident wording on weak evidence.
- **Three strong proposals by default** (was 1-5), never two resting on the same mechanism, candidates acting on the user's stated problem ranked first; more than three strong candidates get one offer to continue.
- **Detail on request:** the three boxes stay the archive's format. A proposal gets them only when the user asks ("detay", "neden", "explain"): the producing skill fills `detail`, sends the record to scenario-critic again and gives the boxes in chat with a prose explanation; `detail` is never rendered, so the card doesn't change.
- **Less reading and output per turn:** archive files are read by `grep` on titles plus the shortlisted blocks, the KPI glossary by `grep` on a name, and a methodology section only when a decision needs it. Chat shows per proposal only the title, one tag line (priority, source, evidence) and the card file; elimination reasons and review reports are given on request.
- **Rules 1, 2, 3, 9 and 19:** every produced scenario is a record and the three boxes are the archive's format and the detailed view (1); the record has exactly one primary KPI, structurally (2); it carries its most important guardrail, and an accessibility-affecting B gets an accessibility warning with a pass/fail criterion that is a pre-start gate (3); the record isn't repeated in chat and a turn produces at most three strong proposals (9); question kind 8 is the >3-candidates offer (19).
- **Rules 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 17 name their gates:** accessibility criteria that fit the element (3); functional equivalence through `invariants`, and which part of the single-variable check is code and which is review (4); the builder's verification, consent (6) and legal-text (11) checks; the archive-pattern label and no tested or proven framing (8); the delivery pipeline, validation to final card (9); no invented chance of winning or uplift (10); review stamps, their fingerprints and their limit (17).
- **Evidence label renamed:** "Arşiv örüntüsü" / "Archive pattern" (was "Arşiv emsali" / "archive precedent"; chat tag "Kanıt: arşiv örüntüsü"); the enum value `archive` is unchanged. The archive is a curated library of test ideas, not a log of tests that won, and no output presents an archive scenario as tested or proven. A block used as written is source `archive`; one fitted to a specific page or form is `adapted` (scenario-critic checks).
- **Warnings upgraded to errors:** a regulated-looking record without a `regulation` warning (the check also reads the invariants and, for strong signals such as KVKK, consent, insurance or pension, the text the mockups show), a guardrail that is the complement of the primary, a difference outside the ring and a colour only in B on an element outside the ring now refuse; before, the record or the card went through with a warning.
- **The coupon example corrected:** `examples/scenario.json` is now an adapted record, "Kupon alanını “İndirim kodum var” bağlantısının arkasına almak geliri artırır mı?", with "Ziyaretçi Başına Gelir (RPV)" as the primary until the business goal is stated (hiding the coupon can move revenue either way), "Kuponlu Sipariş Veren Ziyaretçi Oranı" per assigned visitor as the guardrail protecting coupon use, three invariants, an accessibility warning for the new link and both review stamps.
- **The card is the comparison, nothing else.** A fixed 16:9 card, 1600 × 900: the record's question-form title at the top (at most two lines), then Variant A and Variant B in two equal columns, each a small pill above a large frame, top-aligned so the content lines up; the ring around the changed element and its two-to-four-word label. Frames are as tall as their content (no blank phone screen): a 420px phone screen scaled as a whole by 1.2 (504px on the card, so the phone fills its column and the change reads at a glance; only the frame scales, never text alone), a browser frame filling its column, components at a 15px (phone) or 14px (web) base, nothing below 12px, 44px touch targets on phone. A neutral palette (near-black brand default, grey text and borders, a blue link) replaces the box palette; `card.brand` still takes a shared screen's colors, silently. The ring is a 3px red outline at a 4px offset with no glow, and must not cover neighbouring elements. A Turkish ring label starts with a capital letter; the builder and the validator refuse one that doesn't. The draft marker is a ribbon over a corner of the card. `card.mockup_basis` stays as metadata for the mockup reviewer.
- **What used to be card text goes to chat, once, only when needed:** the "Stronger alternative:" line for a weak test the user asked for (one line under its tag line), a one-line note that a mockup is rebuilt from a description or representative. The brand line is gone: colors come from the screen without a note, and a guide sent later replaces them (rule 12). Masked values carry no note (rule 15).
- **`render_check.mjs` measures the new card, and its errors fail:** a card that isn't 1600 × 900 or overflows, a title over two lines, clipped frame content, a phone frame with blank screen under its content, a ring or label covering screen elements, text below 12px, contrast below 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), A and B frames not top-aligned or not the same width. A frame too small for its stage is a warning; `--strict` makes warnings fail.
- **mockup-reviewer reviews the new card:** only the title, the pills, the mockups and the ring labels as text; A and B large and readable; the ring visible and covering nothing, its label short; no blank phone screen and no misleading clipping; contrast; fidelity to a shared screen's content and colors, or the neutral palette; the manifest as before. It reads the render_check output, and every error is a FIX.
- **Four sample cards through the real pipeline** (validator, scenario-critic, draft, mockup-reviewer, final), one per screen type: the mobile cart coupon example (phone-web, change), a desktop product page (web, move), a native app home screen (phone with a tab bar, add) and a B2B demo form (web, remove). The records are in `examples/` and `examples/samples/`, the final cards and their PNGs in `docs/demo/`.
- **Rules 9, 12 and 15:** the card shows only the comparison and the "Stronger alternative:" line moves to chat (9); colors from a shared screen are used silently, the neutral palette by default, a later guide replaces them (12); masking carries no note on the card (15).

### Fixed in the end-to-end audit
- **Statistics can't come back through the record's text:** the validator refuses statistics language in any record field (p-value, significance, sample size, power, MDE, SRM, CUPED, Bayesian, A/A, ICE scores); the archive's own KPI questions in `detail` are left alone. Win-chance and uplift wording is caught in more forms ("kazanma olasılığı %70", "%12 artar", "yüzde 12 daha fazla").
- **The card can't carry the record either:** `build_card.py` refuses a mockup that draws the record's KPI or guardrail name, or a "Birincil KPI", "Guardrail", "Kanıt:", "Öncelik:" or "Daha güçlü alternatif" line, even when it is identical in both arms.
- **Fewer false refusals, no weaker gates:** a discount or free-shipping element that B shows more no longer demands a guardrail on that benefit (a code or coupon field still always does, any other benefit when B hides, removes or shrinks it); an unchanged element named in A's description no longer counts as the change; the "users who saw it" message now says what to write instead.
- **The regulated flow reaches the user:** the suggest output carries one pre-start gates line (compliance sign-off, accessibility check), since the card shows no warnings. Rule 11 and rule 14 no longer contradict each other: a reason or data-assurance line next to a field that only restates the published privacy notice is produced with a compliance gate, while a change to legal, consent or price text is still held.
- **Flow gaps closed:** a direct `/ab-test-suggest` with a described page stays in suggest and builds on that page; a named test skips the problem question (rule 13); the primary KPI follows the title's outcome, not an archive block's first KPI; a weak explicit ask can carry priority low; the design output has slots for the history reminder, a primary that differs from the user's goal, and removed personal values; pre-registration and decision-rule requests get the out-of-scope line, and the router description now triggers on results and sizing questions.
- **Tall phone screens fit:** `card.phone_scale: 1` keeps a phone frame at 420px instead of 504px, so a form step the test needs whole (fields, consent boxes, button) fits the stage instead of being clipped; render_check's clipping error names it. The end-to-end runs showed every regulated form card clipping at the default scale.
- **Accessibility and rendering gates sharpened:** a modal's warning must name both a visible close control and Esc (one word used to satisfy both), and a bottom sheet or dialog counts as a modal; render_check also reports a ring label clipped by the phone frame's rounded corner.
- **Audit reads less:** `ab-test-audit` reads methodology by section instead of whole, uses rule 5's out-of-scope sentence, adds installment to the price rule and gives Turkish severity tags.
- **Smaller fixes:** the validator refuses an `objection` written in the other language ("Price" in a Turkish record) and the skill templates list both languages' six values; "proven" and "deck-style" left the plugin manifests; the generated bundle header no longer promises statistics; the draft ribbon reads "TASLAK · incelenmedi" (no em dash); Turkish ring labels in the skill examples start with a capital; builder messages say two to four words; `build_card.py` runs under `python3 -I` and writes cards with ordinary file permissions; the KPI glossary gains "Kuponlu Sipariş Veren Ziyaretçi Oranı" (the ambiguous "Kuponlu Sipariş Oranı" kept only as a synonym) and "Hedef Profil Dışı Talep Oranı" for guardrails whose per-order or per-request denominator would shift between arms; mockup-style covers sticky elements, derived figures, carousel dots and text buttons; rule 9's restatement of the delivery pipeline shrank to a pointer.

### Fixed in the final QA pass
- **Invented results and impact are refused wherever they are written:** every free-text field of the record is read for a number tied to an outcome or a chance ("dönüşüm %12 artar", "beklenen artış yüzde 12", "benzer testlerde %12 arttı", "conversion rises 12%", "win probability is 70%", "doubles signups"), in Turkish and English. Page discounts, thresholds, the user's own observed rates, a hypothesis without a number and research quoted with its named source stay allowed. Borderline wording becomes a strict-mode warning for the scenario-critic, whose new claims section decides what code can't.
- **KPI names mean one thing:** a name that encodes a conditional population (per quote, per order, "→") is refused over an assigned-visitor denominator and the reverse, and a glossary name must keep its glossary denominator family. The glossary separates "Sözleşme Oranı (30 gün)" per assigned visitor from the per-quote diagnostic, normalized new subscription value from cash actually collected, and adds the period-refund guardrail ("Dönem Kaynaklı İade veya Şikâyet Talebi Oranı"); 105 canonical names. A post-assignment conditioned metric is never the primary.
- **A move is drawn as a move:** `card.difference` can no longer differ from the record's difference. Each ring is labelled with the position true for its own arm. A pinned element is drawn at the viewport edge, not in the page flow: `.r-fold` splits a phone screen into the first view and a "Kaydırınca görünen kısım" continuation sheet, `.r-sticky` pins the identical element to the bottom of the first view, and `card.mobile_toolbar` draws the browser toolbar and safe area; an in-flow bar labelled as pinned is refused. A move to a later screen is drawn as separate stage phones (`.r-stage-break`, `card.stage_captions`).
- **Clearer cards:** a soft dashed ring (`.hl-soft`) marks a consequence of the variable (prices that follow a default billing period) so the main ring stays on the variable; identical neutral Variant A/B pills; a lighter conditional ribbon; `card.phone_scale` 1.4 for short phone screens and `card.web_scale` 1.2 / 1.4 for short desktop pages; representative URLs use `.example`. render_check adds geometric errors for pill parity, a ring wider than the change, label clearance, sticky geometry, short-screen fill and stage or continuation clipping; mockup-reviewer judges the meaning (ten visual checks, full and half size).
- **Visual check you can trust:** render_check exits 0 `VERIFIED`, 1 `VISUAL_CHECK_FAILED` or 3 `VISUAL_CHECK_UNAVAILABLE` (Playwright or Chromium missing); without a render the reviewer returns `UNVERIFIED`, never PASS, and the user gets the draft as an unverified preview. `--half` writes the half-size PNG on any platform. README gains a Visual check setup section.
- **Shared-page fidelity:** ab-test-design reads the screen once into one inventory and compares Variant A with the screenshot before the critic; logos, decorative icons and a few pixels are represented reasonably, not chased. An icon glyph (`role="img"`) is held to 3:1; a below-floor text colour copied from the user's own screen, identical in both arms and outside the rings, is listed in `card.inherited_contrast` and reported as the page's defect, never as a pass. A new or worse contrast problem in B still fails.
- **Archive reads stay inside one block:** the skills' block read also stops at the next title, and the validator refuses a block not closed by `---`.
- **Screen spec:** a card is now drawn from `card.screen` (the page once, as a node list with page-colour overrides) and `card.change` (one op naming the tested element's id and its ring label); the builder and the validator compile both arms from them, so A and B cannot differ anywhere else and records on one screen share the list verbatim. Raw `variant_a`/`variant_b` markup stays for what the list can't express, and both forms at once are refused.
- **Conditional proposals instead of invented facts or lost ideas:** an optional `assumptions` list separates critical facts (a price display, a field's legal purpose, a step's real name, whether a field is needed later) from visual ones. A record with a critical assumption is medium priority at most and builds a conditional card, never a final one, with an amber "KOŞULLU · doğrulama gerekli" ribbon; the chat lists every critical fact in one "Koşullu (n):" line and asks only the most critical question. When the user confirms, the assumption goes, the record is reviewed again and the final card has no ribbon.
- **Fixable is not blocking:** critic findings are tagged [blocking] or [fixable]; round 1 reports every fixable gap at once, fully specified fixes may be applied directly, and a later round raises no fixable item it could have raised before. Chat findings no longer hold back a record's stamp. The soft signup wall that was dropped in the last audit for a missing Esc criterion now reaches a card.
- **More gates in code:** a complement guardrail written as a transition rate ("Sepetten Ödemeye Geçiş" vs "Sepet Terk"), a "shown to" population, an accessibility warning below 4.5:1 or without a 44×44 pt / 48×48 dp target, a `numeric_change` that names neither a source nor a derivation, a pre-ticked or removed consent box (including "okudum" and opt-in wishes like "… almak istiyorum", now a rule-6 matter), and content clipped at the top of the screen. An added modal can now be drawn (`.r-overlay` holding the ringed `.r-modal`).
- **Shorter turns:** CLAUDE.md is a compact binding layer (23.4 → 13.2 KB), the skills point to it instead of restating rules and the delivery pipeline, and mockup-style.md is split into a core read for every card and sections read on demand. Measured fixed reading per turn: suggest 87.9 → 54.0 KB, design 88.5 → 54.4 KB, audit 40.7 → 24.1 KB. Each proposal in chat is a title, a tag line, a one-sentence "Neden:" and the card file; design prints setup notes only on request; the user's own ideas come first, in the order asked; one question at most, never two in one sentence.

### Kept
- The archive (221 scenarios in 13 stage files), unchanged in content, and the KPI glossary (105 canonical names with definitions and denominators; the RPV row now names the guardrail a change touching a user benefit needs, and "Talep Başına Nitelik Oranı" says that when B changes how many requests arrive, the quality guardrail is the new "Hedef Profil Dışı Talep Oranı" per assigned visitor; likewise coupon use is guarded by the new "Kuponlu Sipariş Veren Ziyaretçi Oranı" per assigned visitor when B changes how many orders arrive).
- The single-variable rule, the three boxes (as the archive's format and the detailed view), ethics, privacy, regulation and accessibility controls, source and evidence labels, the card builder's security allowlist, escaping and drift check, the review gates and fingerprints, and the validators.

## 2.2.0 — 2026-10-10

Built from a full audit after a live run on six screens of a pension and life-insurance app (six scenarios, five critic rounds). Every recurring review finding was traced back to the file that should have prevented it; every engine and builder finding was reproduced with a failing test before it was fixed.

### Breaking
- **Stats verdicts carry a direction.** `decision_code` is `significant_improvement` or `significant_degradation` (the bare `significant` is gone); a significantly worse variant can no longer read as a win. New `--expected-direction increase|decrease`.
- **Guardrail direction is required.** `--ni-margin` without `--guardrail-direction must_not_increase|must_not_decrease` is an error; the old silent default reported a rising harmful rate as clean.
- **`--mde` and `--ni-margin` of 1 or more are rejected** unless written as a percent (`10%`); a bare `1` used to mean a 100% lift.
- **Card mockups go through an allowlist.** Only the tags, attributes and inline-style values the template's components use are accepted; anything else refuses the card.
- **Mockup markup is stricter:** attribute values must be double-quoted, `bottom_nav` is refused on `web` and `phone-web`, and `card.url` is required on those two frames.
- **A pass/fail guardrail needs a `criterion`**, and a scenario box carries 3 to 6 items.

### Fixed
- **Security:** the card builder's deny-list could be bypassed (unquoted event handlers, encoded `javascript:` URIs, `<meta>`, `<form>`, `<style>`); replaced with a strict allowlist. The input scanner missed plain "ignore all previous instructions" lines.
- **Stats:** Holm ran on rounded p-values and could call a non-significant arm significant; `interaction` returned `effect_differs: true` next to a warning not to conclude it; an inconclusive guardrail now reports why and what sample would resolve it; `significance` checks SRM itself; Turkish output no longer leaks English errors.
- **Design flow:** the designer now reads the methodology sections the critic enforces, writes the scenario in full before review, and hands the critic everything it checks. Guardrails state their denominator, lagging guardrails carry their window (`read_after_days`) and the decision rule waits for it, a triggered denominator names an event logged in both arms before the change, and sibling tests on one screen exclude each other.
- **Rules:** a guardrail is never the complement of the primary ("abandonment" now means a later step); accessibility is a pass/fail check closed before traffic, not a segment metric; personal data covers what a screen displays (balances, allocation shares, chart slices, contract numbers), not only what was typed; a defect in Variant A is reported as a fix to ship, never tested and never changed mid-test; the problem question holds the turn; same-kind questions merge into one; the critic loop is capped at two re-reviews; a test the user explicitly asks for is always produced, with its weakness and a stronger alternative stated.
- **Regulated flows:** rule 11 now has two cases. A variant that changes a regulated display is held; a variant inside a regulated flow that leaves regulated displays untouched is produced with compliance sign-off as a pre-start gate and the first sentence of its decision rule.
- **Archive:** 36 guardrails that were the complement of their primary replaced with an independent harm; 35 scenarios given a variable-isolation item; 10 titles that disagreed with their difference type rewritten; 51 generic "read the metric properly" never-do items replaced with variant-scoped ones.
- **Methodology:** ICE bands and the ICE table disagreed on confidence ranges; the fit tiers are renamed Suitable / Conditional / Poor fit so they can't be confused with ICE tiers; every section carries an evidence label.
- **Cards:** the builder validates the scenario before building; a ring may not carry margin or padding; the layout check measured ring labels too small and missed a label covering a stepper digit.

### Added
- Archive: `knowledge/scenarios/finance-pension.md`, 10 scenarios for insurance, pension and finance journeys on web and app (211 → 221 scenarios, 13 stage files), routed from the suggest skill.
- KPI glossary: a finance, insurance and pension section (83 → 99 canonical names), one denominator per row.
- Methodology: pre-start gates, pass/fail guardrails, lagging-guardrail windows, derived personal figures, several scenarios on one screen.
- Stats: guardrail sample planning (`samplesize --ni-margin`, resolvable margin for a given traffic), `--expected-split` for deliberately unequal allocations, A/B/n improvement and degradation flags.
- Results skill: reads a pre-registration block (direction, alpha, allocation, lagging windows, pass/fail checks, pre-start gates) and ships only when the primary is significant in the declared direction, every margin guardrail is clean, every check passed and every window closed.
- Audit skill: checks for regulated areas, protections, sensitive fields, lagging windows, accessibility, complement guardrails, guardrail denominators and concurrent tests.
- Validator: a warning channel with `--strict`; warnings for a missing denominator, a lagging guardrail without a window, a complement guardrail, a missing compliance gate in a regulated scenario and an over-scored Ease.
- Schema: `card.bottom_nav_active`, pre-registration `trigger` and `pre_start_gates`.
- Evals: a regulated flow with personal data on screen, a user-insisted weak test, results read against a pre-registration, an English conversation.

## 2.1.0 — 2026-10-09

Built from seven end-to-end test runs on real pages (insurance quote forms, a product hub, a credit calculator, a car-rental search, a SaaS pricing page, an app onboarding flow, a desktop product page) plus a results/audit dogfood run. Every finding below was reproduced before it was fixed.

### Fixed
- **Stats:** `continuous` no longer reads the first CSV column silently (an id column produced a fake significant result); multi-column files need `--value-column`, pre-period files join by `--id-column`. `samplesize --help` no longer crashes. A repeated single-value flag is an error instead of silently keeping the last value. One-sided tests report a one-sided bound. Units are explicit (`absolute_diff_pp`); an MDE above 1 is read as a percent.
- **Cards:** web cards overflowed into a ~2000px portrait; they now render at 1600x900 with a readable text column. Box headers, role pills and footer follow the user's language (TR/EN). The highlight ring no longer shifts the layout. Phone cards no longer draw an invented app tab bar. Personal data typed into a screenshot's fields is never copied into a card.

### Fixed
- **Security:** `scripts/build_card.py`'s `self_verify` only ever refused a card containing a literal `<script` — `onerror=`, `javascript:`/`vbscript:` URIs, `<iframe>`/`<object>`/`<embed>`, and `data:text/html` URIs all passed through unchecked, contradicting `CLAUDE.md` rule 18's own claim about what gets blocked. Extended the deny-list to cover all of these at the code level, with regression tests for each (including a false-positive test — the event-handler pattern needs a `\b` boundary or it matches inside ordinary words like "content="). Still a deny-list, not a full allowlist HTML sanitizer — noted honestly in the code comment, since an allowlist would need an actual HTML-parsing dependency this repo's stdlib-only convention doesn't have.
- **Methodology:** per-segment result reporting (`ab-test-results`, `ab-test-audit`) now explicitly warns against the "significant in one segment, not in another" fallacy — that's not itself evidence the true effect differs by segment (that needs a formal interaction test, which `analyze_results.py` doesn't compute); segment breakdowns are now framed as exploratory/hypothesis-generating, not a declared per-segment winner.
- **Methodology:** the two-week duration rule's rationale was getting cited inconsistently — `ab-test-results` was explaining a short-but-significant result as "regression-to-the-mean risk" when the rule's actual, documented basis (`methodology.md` → external validity) is business-cycle/temporal coverage. The decision table now names three distinct problems (underpowered-but-complete, mid-test peeking, insufficient temporal coverage) instead of one blended "wait and see."
- **Methodology:** softened the A₁/A₂/B three-arm claim from "does the same verification" as a dedicated A/A test to "provides an embedded A/A diagnostic" — a three-way split affects the primary comparison's power and isn't operationally identical.
- **Methodology:** `CLAUDE.md` rule 9 said manual copy-edit is a fallback "when the script can't be used" while `ab-test-card/SKILL.md` said the opposite for a script error ("don't fall back to building the card by hand") — the two contradicted each other. Removed the fallback allowance from `CLAUDE.md`; a script error means diagnose-and-rerun, not hand-building around every guarantee the script enforces.
- **Methodology:** the ICE score's numeric product (e.g. 336 vs. 392) can read as more precise than it is. `methodology.md` now states explicitly that it's an ordinal heuristic, not a measured expected-value model, and recommends a coarse High/Medium/Low tier when scores are close instead of quoting the product as a tiebreaker.
- **Methodology:** the local-maximum escape tactic (widen a small variable to a whole-page redesign) is a bundled-treatment test, not a rule-4 single-variable test in disguise — it answers "is the new experience better," not "which change did it." Named the distinction (diagnostic experiment vs. radical-redesign experiment) so the two aren't conflated.
- **Audit:** added a checklist item for randomization-unit/analysis-unit mismatch (randomized by user, analyzed by session, breaking the independence assumption the z-test relies on) — a common, easy-to-miss confound that wasn't explicitly checked before.
