# Record checklist

Read before writing or changing a scenario record (`ab-test-design`, `ab-test-suggest`, `ab-test-card`); `CLAUDE.md` is binding and rule numbers point there. The schema (`templates/scenario.schema.json`) is the validator's, never read: a refusal names the field and its fix.

## The skeleton

One JSON file per scenario, written once by the producing skill and started from the skeleton, never from a blank file:

```bash
python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/new_record.py --device <phone|phone-web|web> --difference <change|add|move|remove> --page <stage> --kpi "<glossary name>" --lang <tr|en> [--element <cta|link|field|check|chips|tabs|accordion|text|help|badge|banner>] [--hidden] [--evidence <intuition|sector|archive|user_data|behaviour_data>] [--adapted-from "<archive title>"] [--guardrail "<glossary name>"] [--impact <structural|persuasive|cosmetic>] [--effort <low|medium|high>] [--off-path] [--subtle] --out <record.json>
```

It writes what the validator decides from those facts: the KPI's glossary denominator, the accessibility warning with every criterion, the invariant, the exposure sentence (`--hidden`, tabs or an accordion), `priority.inputs` with their tier and a minimal `card.screen` with the one change. Replace every `⟦…⟧` hint with this page's value (a hint left in is refused) and fill `must_be_true`. `card` may be written with the record (it is outside the critic's fingerprint), but the draft build needs the critic stamp. Added by hand when they apply: `variants[A].is_current: true` (A is the user's own current page); `objection` (`Güven|Fiyat|Uygunluk|Zamanlama|Çaba|Netlik`, or `Trust|Price|Fit|Timing|Effort|Clarity`); `exposure` when the skeleton didn't write it (item 3); `warnings` typed `regulation`, `market`, `feasibility` or `privacy`; `assumptions` (`[{"type": "critical", "text": "<one sentence, at most 200 characters>", "question": "<one short question>"}]`, or `visual` without a question); `numeric_change` (item 9); a guardrail check is `{"kind": "check", "criterion": "…"}` instead of a metric.

## From an archive block

The block's head (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/knowledge/scenarios/FORMAT.md` → Head): its `Etiket:` line's `mercek` is the record's `objection`; `emek` its `priority.inputs.effort` (`düşük` low, `orta` medium, `yüksek` high); `düzey` the kind of change, element level (`görsel`, `gezinme`, `bilgi`, `form`, `metin`, `güven`, `sosyal-kanıt`, `sürtünme`) or structural (`teklif`, `fiyat`, `kişiselleştirme`, `akış`, `elde-tutma`, `onboarding`); `sektör` and `pazar` say where it fits (`pazar=TR` rests on a Turkish practice); `durum=klasik` is a dated pattern. `adapted_from` is always the Turkish `## ` title; an English record takes the block's `EN-Variable:` as its `variable` when there is one.

## Before the first validation

Nearly every first-validation refusal is one of these; the skeleton gets 1 to 4, 6 and 7 right. Write each right the first time, then validate every record of the turn in one call: `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/validate_scenario_json.py <record.json> [more.json]`.

1. **KPI name and denominator are one family.** The glossary row's Payda is the denominator: `{"name": "Dönüşüm Oranı (CR)", "denominator": "Teste atanan ziyaretçi"}`; a session metric counts sessions (`"Hemen Çıkma Oranı"` over `"Sayfayla başlayan oturum"`). Never a count ("Sayısı"), "gören/görüp" or an audience only B has.
2. **An interactive element needs the accessibility warning.** B adds or changes a link, button, field, checkbox, chip, tab, toggle, accordion, menu, modal or carousel → one `accessibility` warning naming all four criteria with their figures: "B yayına girmeden klavye ve ekran okuyucu kontrolünden geçmeli: yeni bağlantı klavyeyle erişilir, ekran okuyucuda görünen adıyla okunur, dokunma alanı en az 44×44 pt, metin kontrastı en az 4.5:1 olur." A modal or pop-up adds focus on open, a visible close control and Esc; an auto-advancing carousel adds a pause.
3. **A conditionally shown element needs `exposure`.** Accordion, tab, modal, pop-up, dropdown, drawer, tooltip or below the fold → who is exposed, with the words "iki kolda" / "both arms": `"exposure": "Kargo akordeonunun başlığı ekrana gelen kullanıcılar; tetikleyici iki kolda aynı noktada kaydedilir."`
4. **A functional element needs `invariants`.** B touches a coupon, discount, payment, cart, filter, search, login, signup or form → 1 to 5 sentences: `["Geçerli bir kod iki kolda da aynı adımla uygulanır."]`
5. **`adapted_from` is the archive's Turkish `## ` title, whole, in an English record too.** Same words and case (straight or curly quotes both match), and `difference` is the block's `Fark:` (değiştir → `change`, ekle → `add`, taşı → `move`, kaldır → `remove`); `archive` also keeps its `Değişken:` as `variable`.
6. **`priority.inputs` has all five, and the tier is the band of their score out of 9.** evidence (`user_data` 3, `behaviour_data` 2, `archive_or_research` 1, `intuition` 0) + impact (`structural` 2, `persuasive` 1, `cosmetic` 0) + `main_path` 1 + `noticeable` 1 + effort (`low` 2, `medium` 1, `high` 0): 7-9 `high`, 4-6 `medium`, 0-3 `low`; `medium` at most with a critical assumption. A native app release alone is `medium` effort; `high` is backend or operational work. `inputs.evidence` follows `evidence.level`: `user_data` → `user_data` or `behaviour_data`; `archive` and `sector` → `archive_or_research`; `intuition` → `intuition`.
7. **`card.change` is one op on an `id` the screen has:** `insert_after`, `insert_before`, `replace`, `remove`, `move_after`, `move_before` or `edit`, each with its ring `label`; a move names both places: `{"op": "move_before", "target": "kupon", "to": "ozet", "label_a": "Kupon altta", "label_b": "Kupon üstte"}`.
8. **Screen nodes keep their shapes.** One kind per node. `{"fold": true}`, `{"stage": true}`, `{"divider": true}` take `true` and nothing else; `align` (`"center"` / `"left"`) only on `h`, `sub`, `text`, `cta`, `link` and `stack`; a `plan` has `name` and `price` as strings: `{"plan": {"name": "Pro", "price": "499 TL", "per": "/ay", "features": ["Sınırsız proje"], "cta": "Başla"}}`; `sticky` only on a screen with a `fold`, placed before it; colours `#rrggbb`, sizes whole numbers.
9. **Nothing differs outside the ring.** Copy, numbers, order and spacing are the same in both arms; a number that differs on purpose carries `numeric_change`, saying where the figure comes from; a consequence the variable forces goes in `card.soft`.
10. **Plain JSON.** Straight `"` around keys and values (curly quotes only inside the text), no trailing comma, no comment.

## Field by field

What the items above and the rules don't already say:

- **Source (rule 8).** `archive`: the block as written, examples localized; `adapted`: its variable and difference type kept, fitted to the user's context; either half changed → `generated`, with `source_note` saying why no block fits and how this differs.
- **Primary KPI (rule 2).** The closest point the title's outcome is still an outcome (a completed order, not a click), name and denominator from the glossary grep: assigned users of each arm, or a trigger logged in both arms before the change renders. An archive block's KPIs are starting points: its first KPI and strongest must-not item stay only where they fit the title and the denominator rule (a coupon test keeps RPV primary until the user states another business goal; a per-quote "Teklif → Sözleşme" → "Sözleşme Oranı (30 gün)" per assigned visitor). A window in the name ("Kayıt Oranı (7 gün)") is part of its definition; the denominator names the event it counts from, one both arms share (assignment, first open). A change to what is charged (a price, a discount amount, a bundle) takes a revenue-based primary.
- **Guardrail (rule 3).** A legal or product window is named by its definition ("Cayma Oranı (yasal cayma süresi içinde)"), never guessed. A functional protection that matters more is a check with its criterion. When B touches a user benefit (a coupon, a discount, free shipping, refunds), the guardrail watches the benefit, because hiding a coupon field can raise revenue when code holders pay full price: "Kuponlu Sipariş Veren Ziyaretçi Oranı" per assigned visitor, plus the functional pass criterion in `invariants` (a valid code is entered, applied and discounted, and errors are handled, identically in both arms).
- **Invariants** are omitted when B touches no link, field, coupon, payment, search, filter or form; `numeric_change` also covers an amount computed from the page (the sum left to free shipping).
- **Warnings:** at most three, one sentence each: `accessibility` (item 2); `regulation` for rule 11 (b) ("Compliance must approve B's exact copy before the test takes traffic."); `market`, `feasibility`, `privacy` naming the dependency.
- **Assumptions (rule 10):** at most three. `critical` (with `question`) instead of inventing the fact or dropping a strong idea, the rationale saying what it waits for; `visual` (no question) for representative names, photos and decoration, usually omitted.
- **Evidence and priority (rules 9, 10).** `evidence.note` says what the record rests on and, for `intuition` or `sector`, why it's low-confidence, naming a source the user gave; no refused claim anywhere in the record. `impact` is the change's tier in the impact ranking (the offer, a step or missing decision information `structural`; hierarchy, objection copy or a trust signal `persuasive`; colour, radius, font or generic wording `cosmetic`); `effort` medium for front-end work or a native app release, high for backend or operational work (`methodology.md` → Prioritization).
- **`must_be_true` (rule 9):** the belief that must hold on this page for B to work (at most 160 characters) and one cheap, qualitative probe (session recordings, field analytics, five user tests, support tickets), never a sample size or duration (rule 5): `{"belief": "Kodsuz kullanıcı açık kutuyu görünce kod aramaya çıkıyor.", "probe": "Sepetten çıkıp dönen oturumların kayıtlarına bakın."}`.
- **A record the user brings as text** is mapped as given: `source: generated` with a `source_note` saying the user brought it, `evidence.level` as the user stated (`intuition` if nothing), no invented `objection`; a missing required piece routes to `ab-test-design`, one sentence saying what was missing.
