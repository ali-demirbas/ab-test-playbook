# render_check and the frame

Read for a `render_check.mjs` error or warning, a question about a frame's scale, or what the template draws (the core: `knowledge/mockup-style.md`).

## Frame scale

Components are drawn at their sizes and never scaled on their own (no `zoom`, `transform` or smaller text to make content fit); only a frame as a whole is scaled, and the builder picks that scale, never the author. A phone has one size, `zoom: 1.2` (420px drawn 504px wide), and keeps it whenever the screen fits; a screen too long for it is drawn smaller (1.1, then 1, then the size that fits down to 0.93, where the smallest text is still 12px) and one that would leave most of the stage empty larger (1.3 or 1.4), so phones come in a few sizes across a deck. A browser frame always fills its column, and its scale (0.93 to 1.4) is the largest at which the page still fits the stage. The builder estimates the layout from the template's own sizes, so the choice is the same on every machine; render_check still measures the real render. `card.phone_scale` and `card.web_scale` (1, 1.2 or 1.4) remain as an override and are not needed. A screen that still clips at the smallest scale is too long: tighten the drawing (a label and its field in one block, `.r-steps` for a step indicator) or use a fold; never cut away an element the test or its regulation warning is about. Stage phones (`special-cases.md`) are never rescaled. A phone frame is 420px wide; a desktop browser frame fills its column (about 728px).

## Card layout

- **Layout:** 1600 × 900, fixed; the template sets the title, pills, columns, background and the system font stack (no font file, so the card opens offline). `card.theme: "dark"` darkens the ground, title and pills for dark slides; the mockups keep the page's colours.
- **Neutral palette** (no shared screen, no guide; rule 12): text `#111827`, secondary `#374151`, muted `#6b7280`, borders `#e5e7eb` / `#d1d5db`, surfaces `#ffffff` / `#f9fafb`, link `#2563eb`, success `#15803d`, warning `#b45309`; `--brand` `#1f2937` on `--on-brand` `#ffffff`. A page's or guide's colors: Brand.

What the template draws, for reference (the producer only fills the mockups): card 1600 × 900, padding 40px 48px 32px, background a soft `#f7f8fa` → `#eef0f3` gradient, font stack `-apple-system, "Segoe UI", Inter, Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`. Title 30px / 1.25 / weight 650, `#111827`, in a box two lines tall on every card and set at its bottom (a one-line title sits on the pills), so the pills (y=127) and the frames (y=171) never move between cards. Stage: two equal columns with a 48px gap, each a pill above its frame, frames centred in their column and top-aligned. Pills: A and B identical (white, 1px `#d1d5db` border, `#111827` text, 16px / 700, fully rounded); only the text differs, never a winner color. Phone frame: white screen, 1px `#d1d5db` border, radius 36px, soft shadow, 14px status bar at 9:41 with a camera island; the active tab drawn in `--brand`. Photo places (`.ph.img`): a light gradient with a picture icon. Ring label: a `#e11d48` pill with white text, 14px / 700, fully rounded. `card.theme: "dark"`: ground `#0f172a`, title `#f1f5f9`, slate pills, continuation captions `#cbd5e1`; frames, rings and labels as on the light card.

## render_check

`node scripts/render_check.mjs <card.html>` renders a card in headless Chromium (optional: needs Node and Playwright with Chromium); `--shot` writes `card-<name>.png`, `--strict` makes warnings fail too. The final build runs the same render for its own PNG: it writes `card-<name>.png` next to the card and prints its path, or says in one line that Node or Playwright is missing and still succeeds (`--no-png` skips it). Errors: the card isn't exactly 1600 × 900 or something overflows it; the title runs over two lines; frame, stage-phone or continuation content is clipped, or a continuation sheet isn't its screen's width; a phone frame shows more than 24px of blank screen; a ring label overlaps anything outside its ring or sits under 6px from what it faces (the gap is reported), or a ring overlaps text or painted elements outside it; a ring too wide (over 45% of its screen, or wider than 85% and taller than 40%); an `.r-sticky` not touching the bottom of its screen, or a pinned label outside one; pills that differ in anything but text; text below 12px or below 4.5:1 contrast (3:1 for large text); A and B frames not top-aligned or not the same width. Warnings (errors in `--strict`): a short single phone that a larger scale would fill (rare: the builder enlarges a short screen itself); a frame using under 40% of the stage height or (web) under 45% of the column width. It can't judge meaning (a gap lost inside a ring wrapper, a label on a 1px line, a label false for its arm); the mockup reviewer reads its output and checks those. The faded continuation under a first view is frame, not mockup, and isn't measured; a share image is shot at 1200 × 630 and checked for its size, overflow and cut-off frames only.
